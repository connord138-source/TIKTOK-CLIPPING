# Clipper networks & agencies — research (2026-09-23)

The user wants into the agency/network structure (Lumina, Propaganda, etc.) on top of
open marketplace campaigns. Landscape from live sites + industry coverage.

## The three tiers of the clipping economy

**Tier 1 — Open marketplaces (we're here):** Whop Content Rewards public bounties.
No gate, instant start, pay per verified view from campaign pools. Also: Reach.cat,
Vyro, Clipify, clipping.net (unvetted; Whop remains the deepest).

**Tier 2 — Agency-run networks/communities (the ask):** agencies that aggregate
brand budgets and push campaigns to their clipper pools. Key finding: **most run
their clipper side through Whop/Discord communities anyway.**
- **Clipping Culture** — whop.com/clippingculture. 510,290 members, 4.7★ (3,149
  reviews), NPR + Business Insider coverage. Claims 500k clipper network, 10B+ views.
  ⭐ We're ALREADY posting their campaigns (CoD MW4, Crazy Taxi, DDI are theirs).
  Joining their whop = their campaign feed + community. Join button on page.
- **Propaganda** — propgda.com ("Clipping Agency for Brands"). 300k+ "performance-
  verified" creators, real brand roster (ByteDance/Dreamina, PGF), claims $952,245
  paid to clippers on one campaign, 72.6M-view case study. "Become A Clipper" =
  whop.com/checkout/plan_QcMZ4pQ1aoCcy (price not visible without opening checkout —
  CHECK PRICE; if it's a paid membership, judge whether it clearly gates real
  campaigns. Never pay to work without visible campaign flow.)
- **Clip.tech** — Discord-based clipper community. Low public info.
- **Shuffle Clipping** — whop application (already on our list, $25k pool, exact niche).
- **Clipping Agency (clippingagency.co)** — Whop-based vetted network, gaming lane.

**Tier 3 — Managed/application networks & retainers (the ceiling):**
- **Lumina (luminaclippers.com/apply):** apply with links to an account that already
  posts short-form ("doesn't need a large following") → manual review → campaign
  briefs. **No fee, no contract, you keep your accounts.** Pays settled rate per
  verified view ON TOP of whatever your own platform monetization pays ("paid
  twice"). $1–5 CPM band. Works crypto/gaming/SaaS/personal brands.
- **Direct streamer deals:** the top of the market — creators hire clip armies
  directly (N3on: $1.4M to 303 clippers in 5 weeks). Elite clippers get
  **$500–$1,500/mo retainers + bonuses** (CreatorDB). Entry = track record.

## Honest notes
- **"Paid twice" is mostly future upside for us:** TikTok's own monetization needs
  10k followers + 100k views/30d and >1min videos (our clips are shorter). Stream
  one ≈ $0 until the account matures. Don't pick a network for that promise.
- **Approval rate is the industry-wide filter** (Lumina's own data: 44% approval on
  one campaign; rejects earn $0). Our per-campaign compliance recipes are the moat.
- Agencies want accounts that already post. **Applications land better after our
  first posting week** — the 5 banked clips + live posts + early numbers ARE the
  portfolio.
