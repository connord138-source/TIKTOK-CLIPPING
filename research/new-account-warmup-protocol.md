# New-account warm-up protocol (TikTok + IG Reels)

Source: Coinbase One Shot campaign's Warm Up Doc (Notion, pulled 2026-09-23). Required
by that campaign before posting; sensible hygiene for @chat.clip.that generally.
Account created 2026-09-22 → day counting starts from first compliant scroll day.

**Setup first:** profile 100% complete (real-ish photo, bio, niche-relevant username ✓),
2FA on, valid email, NO link in bio yet, first 10+ posts from the mobile app only
(no web/scheduler/API).

**Auto-flags to avoid:** posting within 24h of creation; day-1 bio links; mass
follow/unfollow; engaging only one narrow niche; multiple posts before any
engagement; following everyone back; emoji-only comments.

- **Day 1 — pure scroll:** 30+ min FYP; watch videos through; like 15–20 niche videos
  spread out; 5–7 genuine word comments; follow 8–10 niche accounts; save 2–3. No
  posting, no links.
- **Day 2 — train the algorithm:** repeat day 1; search niche keywords directly;
  watch 10+ niche videos fully; engage a few non-niche videos too. Still no posting.
- **Day 3 — ONE warm-up post:** non-promotional niche content, trending sound from
  last 7 days, unique footage (not recycled), posted from mobile app. Then wait 24h.
- **The test:** 1k+ views → cleared, post freely. Under 200 → account flagged; start
  over (new account).

**After warm-up:** keep 5–10 min daily niche engagement; first 10 posts via mobile
app; add bio link only after consistent views.
