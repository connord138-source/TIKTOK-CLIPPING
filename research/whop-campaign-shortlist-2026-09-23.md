## Common compliance patterns (QC checklist inputs)
1. FTC disclosure `#Ad` first hashtag/own line, or campaign hashtag.
2. Mandatory brand @-tag in caption; sometimes follow-requirements.
3. ONLY provided source folders — never scrape the creator's socials.
4. ≥7–10s length, ≥30 days live, likes visible, ≥0.20% ER, no paid promotion,
   ≤5 reposts of same video.
5. Audio rules vary: trailer-audio-only (CoD) / no BG music (Crazy Taxi) /
   copyright-free only (DD) / music-ok (Candid). `clipper.py cut --no-loudnorm`
   exists for strict-audio campaigns.
6. Logo watermark required on brand campaigns → `clipper.py cut --logo <png>`.
7. AI label per CLAUDE.md rule 4: ON only when a clip contains an added AI element.
   No shortlisted campaign prohibits the label.
