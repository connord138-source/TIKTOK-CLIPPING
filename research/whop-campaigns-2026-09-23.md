Method: browsed `contentrewards.com/discover` (Whop's public Content Rewards campaign
browser) headless in-container, Clipping filter + TikTok platform. Pulled the public JSON
API (`/api/campaign/campaigns/discover[?/{id}]`) for 146 unique campaigns, fetched full
detail for 28, and read the posting-rules Google Docs / Notion pages for every finalist.
Raw data snapshot: `research/whop-campaign-data-2026-09-23.json`. **Nothing was joined,
no applications submitted.** Numbers move daily — re-check "remaining" before first clip.

Context: shortlist is for **@chat.clip.that** — brand-new TikTok, 0 followers, general
streamer/gaming clip identity, US-based. Hard filters applied: TikTok allowed · no
follower/account-age minimum · true clipping (not UGC-from-scratch).

Payout mechanics (applies to all): CPM = $ per 1,000 views on your post. "Min payout" is
the smallest claimable amount → effective minimum views for money = minPayout ÷ rate ×
1000. "Max payout" caps earnings per submission. "Paid out" below = budget spent to
clippers per the API (`budgetSpentCents`).

## Common compliance patterns (bake into clipper.py QC)
1. FTC disclosure `#Ad` first hashtag/own line (CoD, DD, Crazy Taxi) or campaign hashtag (#TinderPartner, #forgeguipartner).
2. Mandatory @-tag of brand account in caption; sometimes follow-requirements (Valorant: 4 accounts).
3. Only provided source folders — never scrape the creator's socials.
4. ≥7–10s length, ≥30 days live, visible likes, ≥0.20% ER, no paid promotion/story boosting, ≤5 reposts of same video across pages.
5. Audio rules vary per campaign: trailer-audio-only (CoD), no BG music (Crazy Taxi), copyright-free only (DD), music-ok (Candid, Alpha).
6. Watermark/logo overlays required on brand campaigns (CoD, Crazy Taxi, Alpha) — add a logo-overlay pass to the ffmpeg chain.
7. AI-generated label stays ON (our rule) — no campaign above prohibits it.
