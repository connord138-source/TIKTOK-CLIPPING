# Privacy Policy — ChatClipThat Drafts

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

ChatClipThat Drafts ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer and
connects only to the owner's own TikTok account.

## What the app accesses
With the account owner's permission, the app receives from TikTok:
- basic profile information for the connected account (such as its open ID and display name);
- access and refresh tokens that let the app upload videos to that account's drafts;
- the processing status of videos the app uploaded;
- if the owner turns on the optional stats permission: the account's follower and like counts
  and the public statistics of its own videos.

## How it is used
This information is used only to upload the owner's videos to their TikTok drafts, to show
upload status, and to show the owner statistics about their own account.

## Where it is stored
Tokens and settings are stored in files on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any
server other than TikTok's own API, and they are not sold or shared with anyone.

## Removing access and data
The owner can disconnect TikTok inside the app, which deletes the stored tokens, and can
remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app
permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data.

## Other people's data
The app does not collect data about any other TikTok users.

## Changes and contact
This policy may be updated by editing this page. Questions can be raised by opening an issue
on the GitHub repository that hosts this page.
