# Terms of Service — ChatClipThat Drafts

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

ChatClipThat Drafts ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It
lets the owner upload video files they have prepared to the drafts/inbox of their own TikTok
account, where they review, caption and publish them in the TikTok app.

## Who can use it
The app is operated by its owner for their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public
and has no other users.

## What the app does
- Signs in to TikTok with the owner's permission (Login Kit).
- Uploads video files chosen by the owner to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts
  (Content Posting API, `video.upload`).
- Checks the status of those uploads.

The app never publishes a video on its own. Publishing always happens in the TikTok app, by
the account owner.

## Your responsibilities
The account owner is responsible for the content they upload and publish, including having
the rights to use it, and for following TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

changes its services.
